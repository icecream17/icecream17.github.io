Theorem dvdsprmquot 51372
Description: The positive divisors of 𝑁 not divisible by a prime divisor 𝑃 are unchanged when 𝑁 is divided by 𝑃. (Contributed by Zhi Wang, 5-Nov-2026.)
Assertion
Ref Expression
dvdsprmquot ⊢ ((𝑃 ∈ ℙ ∧ 𝑃 ∥ 𝑁) → {𝑗 ∈ {𝑘 ∈ ℕ ∣ 𝑘 ∥ 𝑁} ∣ ¬ 𝑃 ∥ 𝑗} = {𝑗 ∈ {𝑘 ∈ ℕ ∣ 𝑘 ∥ (𝑁 / 𝑃)} ∣ ¬ 𝑃 ∥ 𝑗})
