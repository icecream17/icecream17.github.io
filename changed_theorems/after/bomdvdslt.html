Theorem bomdvdslt 51411
Description: The prime Omega function increases strictly along proper divisibility of positive integers. If one nonzero integer divides another of different absolute value, the same strict inequality follows by applying this theorem to their absolute values using absdvdsb 16489, dvdsabsb 16490, and bomabs 51399. The positive-integer form applies directly to the prime divisors in bomnn1 51412. (Contributed by Zhi Wang, 27-Oct-2026.)
Assertion
Ref Expression
bomdvdslt ⊢ (((𝑀 ∈ ℕ ∧ 𝑁 ∈ ℕ) ∧ (𝑀 ∥ 𝑁 ∧ 𝑀 ≠ 𝑁)) → (Ω‘𝑀) < (Ω‘𝑁))
