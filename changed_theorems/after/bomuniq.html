Theorem bomuniq 51416
Description: Characterize the signed prime Omega function among complex-valued functions on the nonzero rationals by complete additivity and the value one on primes. Prime factorization determines the values on positive integers. Squaring a nonzero numerator makes it positive and doubles its function value. The numerator and denominator then determine the value at any nonzero rational. For the alternate version restricted to integer-valued functions, which is the homomorphism characterization stated in df-bom 51381, see bomuniq2 51417. (Contributed by Zhi Wang, 20-Nov-2026.)
