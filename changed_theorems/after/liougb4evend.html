Theorem liougb4evend 51607
Description: The double of a positive even integer has a negative-negative pair for Liouville's function. Apply liougb4dvds 51606 to the double, which is divisible by four. (Contributed by Zhi Wang, 20-Oct-2026.)
Hypotheses
Ref Expression
liougb4evend.1 ⊢ (𝜑 → 𝐴 ∈ ℕ)
liougb4evend.2 ⊢ (𝜑 → 2 ∥ 𝐴)
Assertion
Ref Expression
liougb4evend ⊢ (𝜑 → ∃𝑎 ∈ ℕ ∃𝑏 ∈ ℕ ((2 · 𝐴) = (𝑎 + 𝑏) ∧ (λ‘𝑎) = -1 ∧ (λ‘𝑏) = -1))
Distinct variable group:   𝐴,𝑎,𝑏
Allowed substitution hints:   𝜑(𝑎, 𝑏)
