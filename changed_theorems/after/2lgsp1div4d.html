Theorem 2lgsp1div4d 51486
Description: If 𝑃 is prime and one quarter of (𝑃 + 1) is even, then two is a quadratic residue modulo 𝑃. (Contributed by Zhi Wang, 17-Oct-2026.)
Hypotheses
Ref Expression
2lgsp1div4d.1 ⊢ (𝜑 → 𝑃 ∈ ℙ)
2lgsp1div4d.2 ⊢ (𝜑 → 2 ∥ ((𝑃 + 1) / 4))
Assertion
Ref Expression
2lgsp1div4d ⊢ (𝜑 → (2 /L 𝑃) = 1)
