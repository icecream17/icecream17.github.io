Theorem liouoddval 51546
Description: The function 𝐹 specified by the equality hypothesis selects a Liouville value or its reflected negative according to the displayed remainder comparison. For a positive integer modulus 𝑃, let 𝑟 be the least nonnegative residue of the integer argument: use (λ‘𝑟) below (𝑃 / 2) and -(λ‘(𝑃 − 𝑟)) at or above it. See liouoddval1d 51550 for the first case. We call this piecewise construction the odd completion. For positive odd 𝑃 it reverses sign under negation at every integer by liouoddnegd 51557. The residue function of lioumodval 51520 instead uses (λ‘𝑟) throughout the residue interval.

Here the positive central interval is the open interval from zero to (𝑃 / 2), and its positive integer points are the positive central representatives. We call the open interval from (𝑃 / 4) to (𝑃 / 3) the middle band, and the open interval from (𝑃 / 3) to (𝑃 / 2) the upper third. The latter is the top third of the positive central interval. These names are local conventions for the doubling and tripling comparisons; see sub2bndd 51310 and liou2refl3d 51544. If 𝑃 is a prime greater than three and twice 𝑃 has no positive-positive pair, the completion is multiplicative away from multiples of 𝑃 by liouoddmuld 51593. (Contributed by Zhi Wang, 20-Oct-2026.)
