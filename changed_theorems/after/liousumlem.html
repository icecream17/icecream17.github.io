Theorem liousumlem 51478
Description: Lemma for liousum 51479. When the prime 𝑃 divides the positive integer 𝑁, the divisor sums of Liouville's function at 𝑁 and (𝑁 / 𝑃) add to the sum over divisors of 𝑁 not divisible by 𝑃. The bijection dvdsquotf1o 51356 pairs the remaining divisors with those of the quotient. By liouprm 51443 and lioumul 51448, the paired divisors have opposite values. (Contributed by Zhi Wang, 5-Nov-2026.)
