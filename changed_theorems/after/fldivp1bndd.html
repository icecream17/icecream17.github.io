Theorem fldivp1bndd 51324
Description: For a positive integer 𝐴 and a real number 𝐵 greater than one, adding one to the floor of (𝐴 / 𝐵) gives a positive integer at most 𝐴. Multiplying this integer by 𝐵 gives a number strictly greater than 𝐴. (Contributed by Zhi Wang, 17-Oct-2026.)
Hypotheses
Ref Expression
fldivp1bndd.1 ⊢ (𝜑 → 𝐴 ∈ ℕ)
fldivp1bndd.2 ⊢ (𝜑 → 𝐵 ∈ ℝ+)
fldivp1bndd.3 ⊢ (𝜑 → 1 < 𝐵)
Assertion
Ref Expression
fldivp1bndd ⊢ (𝜑 → (((⌊‘(𝐴 / 𝐵)) + 1) ∈ ℕ ∧ ((⌊‘(𝐴 / 𝐵)) + 1) ≤ 𝐴 ∧ 𝐴 < (𝐵 · ((⌊‘(𝐴 / 𝐵)) + 1))))
